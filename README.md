# BotShuttleRespawn
- ONLY WORKS IN MULTIPLAYER because respawn shuttle doesn't exist in singleplayer mode. Maybe i'll try making a singleplayer one eventually, as of now if you're playing solo just use a private server.
- Maybe unstable (idk, but use it at your own risk)
- No newgame needed
- Now your bots will respawn with you or any other palyer that calls the respawn shuttle, beware that they are dumb and you need to order them to follow you to make them leave the shuttle
- Bots also respawn on level transition, meaning: entering/leaving outposts, reaching landmarks on the campaign map
- Bots will be subjected to skill drain just like normal players everytime they respawn, skill drain is the same as the one you configure in the lobby
- Bots respawn with basic kits based on their roles just like player characters (bots will not spawn with advanced gear ex: security officers respawning with smgs/shotguns and body armor)

- This is my first mod so expect bugs, also this mod was based on another mod called "respawnablebots"
- Please report any bugs or funny cheeses you find with this mod
